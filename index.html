What the system does

Each feature removes a manual step from the daily attendance routine.

Daily marking

Teachers open a class list and mark Present, Absent, or Late in one pass. Records are date-stamped, and duplicate sessions are prevented.

Automatic percentages

Attendance is calculated per student, per subject, and per month, so no one has to total a register by hand.

Shortage alerts

Students below the minimum (75% by default) are flagged to the teacher and see a warning on their dashboard.

Leave requests

Students submit leave with a reason. Approved leave is recorded as excused and excluded from the shortage calculation.

Reports and export

Administrators generate class, subject, and defaulter reports and download them as PDF or CSV for audits.

Role-based access

Admins, teachers, and students each see what they need. Edits are logged with who changed a record and when.

How a day of attendance flows

From the first lecture to the monthly report.

  1. Teacher signs in and selects the class

    The dashboard lists today's timetable slots for that teacher.

  2. Attendance is marked

    The class list loads with everyone set to Present. The teacher changes only the exceptions and saves.

  3. The system validates and stores

    Duplicate sessions are blocked, approved leave is applied, and the record is written to the database.

  4. Percentages and alerts update

    Totals are recalculated and anyone below the threshold is flagged immediately.

  5. Students and admins view results

    Students check their own record. Admins export reports for the department.

System design

A three-tier web application with a relational database.

Architecture

Presentation: browser interface for teacher, student, and admin.
Application: REST API with authentication, attendance rules, and report generation.
Data: relational database holding users, classes, sessions, and attendance.

Who uses it

Three roles with separate permissions.

Administrator

  • Manage users, classes, and subjects
  • Set the minimum attendance threshold
  • Generate and export reports

Teacher

  • Mark and correct attendance
  • Approve or reject leave
  • See defaulters for their subjects

Student

  • View attendance by subject and month
  • Apply for leave
  • Receive shortage warnings

Scope and outcomes

Problems solved

Manual registers are slow to total, easy to alter, and hard to audit. This system makes attendance easier to track and helps students learn about shortages early.
